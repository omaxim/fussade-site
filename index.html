    How the two methodologies were linked

    A Slovenian certificate and a Czech one do not measure the same thing: a different reference floor area, a local climate against one reference year, other ventilation and temperature assumptions, other hot-water and lighting defaults, and a different rule for a heat pump's ambient heat. Before training, every Slovenian certificate value was re-expressed in the Czech definitions from the two methodology texts alone: PURES 2010 with TSG-1-004 and TSG-2022 on one side, vyhláška 264/2020 with ČSN 73 0331-1 on the other. Below, that chain for the median building of a chosen kind and era. Pick one and hover a step.

    Medians of the Slovenian register per group, passed through the same code that built the training targets (fussade.transpose). The chain is exact for that median building; per building the factors depend on era, kind and the building's own system efficiency.

    How good is the model, and where does it come from

    Trained on the Slovenian energy-certificate register only, on cadastral attributes every Czech building also has: year, storeys, footprint, construction, use, climate. Its targets were re-expressed in Czech definitions from the two national methodologies alone; how that is done is shown below. What it outputs is a posterior distribution per building, not a number. Below, that posterior against the certificate value on Slovenian buildings the model never saw in training: each column is the stacked posterior of every building whose certificate falls in that band, so a column reads as p(prediction | certificate value). The dashed line is perfect agreement. Hover for the numbers.
